The waves

A wave is an admission gate, not a barrier: a ticket starts the moment its dependencies are merged and an owner slot is free. Content and the human tasks start at hour zero and go straight to main.

Seats

Every agent runs in its own c11 tab Atin can watch. The fleet runs on Hyperion; the board is a local Lattice board in this checkout. No CI gate: the review and the 10-second gate are the protection.

Orchestrator

Opus, high. Admission, the board, review triggers, the report. Writes no feature code.

Merge Captain

Opus, high. Runs the gate at the exact head and squash-merges reviewed PRs to main.

Ticket owners

Luna (gpt-6-luna), max effort, fast mode. Every code ticket. Two failed review rounds go to a design pass.

Two reviewers

Opus (high) and Astra (gpt-6-astra, xhigh), in parallel on the same head, read-only.

Design pass

Opus, high. Only for a recurring class of finding, or verification failing twice.

Result Validator

Opus, high, fresh, after the last v1.0 merge: EVALUATION's terminal audit, scoped to v1.0.

The team

Content, the human tasks and T23's runs, with their own tools. People push content and runs straight to main.

Caps

Owners: 8 in waves 1 to 3. One landing slot; two PRs waiting stops admissions. Thresholds to look in: S 45, M 90, L 150 minutes.

Human tasks

Work only a person can do. The Orchestrator never waits on these except where a task blocks something, and a human checkpoint never pauses landings.
