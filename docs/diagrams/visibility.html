Who sees what

Each role sees only its row of one table. In the story: the monitor never sees the meeting city or the cell's playbook, the cell member who reads the story never sees the city, and nobody in the game sees whether a story is genuine. The code enforces the table by structure, so a mistake turns into an error you see, not a leak you don't.
